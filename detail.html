Case Study

 

AFTERHOURS - Reimagining Alternative Fashion

 

 

Afterhours is an independent fashion label built for those who refuse to fit the mold. Hex & Hollow partnered with the emerging brand to develop a distinctive visual identity that captures its rebellious spirit and transforms it into a cohesive, recognizable brand experience.

Project Challenge

Afterhours had a strong creative vision but lacked a consistent visual identity to distinguish itself in a crowded alternative fashion market. Its existing branding struggled to communicate the brand's personality across social media, promotional materials, and digital platforms.

 

OUR SOLUTION - We developed a bold visual system combining distressed typography, high-contrast photography, and a palette of black, bone white, and deep crimson. From a custom logo and brand guidelines to social media templates and a redesigned website, every touchpoint was crafted to reflect the brand's unconventional identity.

Visual Media Placeholder

Results and Key Outcomes

The rebrand established a cohesive visual presence across digital and print platforms, strengthened brand recognition, and gave Afterhours the tools to communicate consistently with its target audience. The result was a distinctive creative identity that felt authentic to the brand and resonated with its alternative fashion community.
